Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Data controller

The controller is the operator of the holiday home Rifugio Tavolara, Villaggio li piri, ground floor, 07052 San Teodoro (SS), Italy, CIN IT090092C2000S4969 — contact: rifugiotavolara.st@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you send the booking request form we collect only what you type:

  • name, email address and phone number;
  • arrival and departure dates, number of guests, optional message;
  • the language you used to fill in the form.

We never collect payment data on this site and we do not profile you or run advertising.

3. Why we process it

Your data is used only to answer your request and, if we confirm it, to run the booking (pre-contractual steps and performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to comply with the tax and legal duties linked to the stay (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Where it is stored

Data lives in the Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database of the villa-serenita-san-teodoro project, hosted in a European region (eur3). Access is restricted to the operator through authentication: nothing is public and nobody can read it without authorisation.

5. How long we keep it

  • Requests that are not confirmed: deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Confirmed bookings: kept for as long as needed to run the stay and for the periods required by tax rules.

6. Cookies and third parties

This site uses no profiling cookies and no analytics. The technical services it relies on (fonts and the map) are described in the cookie policy.

7. Your rights

You may ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and for data portability, by writing to rifugiotavolara.st@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

If this policy changes we update the date at the top; changes apply from publication.
