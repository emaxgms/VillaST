Cookie policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies and local browser storage are small files a site can save on your device to work properly or to recognise you. Some are technical (necessary), others profile or measure user behaviour.

2. What this site uses

No profiling cookies, no advertising cookies, no analytics. You will not see a consent banner because there is nothing to consent to: the site does not track visitors.

The reserved area /admin, used only by the operator, relies on the browser's technical storage (Firebase login) required to sign in: it is strictly functional and does not involve visitors.

3. Third-party services

Pages load technical resources from external services, which receive your IP address to deliver them:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) — the site's typefaces;
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — the calendar library;
  • Google Maps — the map is embedded and loads only when you scroll to it; at that point Google may set its own cookies;
  • Google Firebase — hosting and database of the site.

We have no direct control over these services: see their policies (Google, jsDelivr).

4. If we add statistics later

If we enable an analytics tool (for example aggregate visit statistics), we will update this page and ask for consent where required, before switching it on.

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge), usually under "Privacy and security". Blocking third-party resources does not prevent booking: the form still works.

6. Contact

Any question about this page: rifugiotavolara.st@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
