Welcome

The Retreat

Rifugio Tavolara is situated in San Teodoro city centre, offering easy access to key attractions. Cala d’Ambra Beach is 1.6 km away, while Isola di Tavolara lies 15 km from the property.

Guests enjoy a private beach area, lush garden, and spacious terrace. The apartment features air-conditioning, a fully equipped kitchenette, and free on-site private parking.

The apartment comprises two bedrooms, one bathroom, and a comfortable living room. Amenities include a washing machine, dining area, and outdoor furniture, ensuring a pleasant stay.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the beach?

Cala d’Ambra is 1.5 km away, La Cinta 2 km and Isuledda 2.3 km — a few minutes by car, with free private parking at the property.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00 to 18:00, check-out from 08:00 to 11:00. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

How many guests can the apartment sleep?

Up to 4 guests: 1 double bed, 1 bunk bed and 1 sofa bed. A cot is always available free of charge for children aged 0–2.

Is there parking?

Yes: free private parking on site, no reservation needed.

Are bed linen and towels included?

They are available on request, with a surcharge. The apartment also has a washing machine for guests.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, on request and possibly with a surcharge. Just mention it in your booking message.

How do I pay for the booking?

You get a confirmation by email or WhatsApp within 24 hours; payment is due before arrival by bank transfer.
